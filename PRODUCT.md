# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary: decision-makers at small and mid-size businesses — brands, startups, local businesses — looking for a creative + growth partner. Initial local markets: Hindupur, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and surrounding regions. Initial international markets: UAE, UK, USA, Canada, Australia.
- Their job: quickly understand what the agency is, which services fit, whether it can help, and how to start an engagement (enquiry / proposal / consultation).

## Product Purpose

Katha Kantam Media is transforming from a traditional content creation agency into a 360° Creative, Digital Growth & Business Technology Agency.

The website is the client acquisition and business development platform, not an online portfolio. Visitors should immediately understand who they are, what makes them different, which services and solutions they provide, which industries they specialize in, how they solve business problems, why clients should trust them, and how to contact / request a quote / schedule a consultation.

Primary success signal: visitors move into genuine business opportunities (enquiry → proposal → consultation).

## Positioning

360° Creative, Digital Growth & Business Solutions.

Tagline: CREATE • MARKET • AUTOMATE • GROW.

## Operating Context

- Web app: React 18 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS + lucide-react; deploy target Vercel. Enquiry backend: Supabase.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Eight capability areas:
  A. Creative & Media Production
  B. Branding & Creative Strategy
  C. Digital Marketing & Growth
  D. Website & Digital Experience Development
  E. AI & Business Automation
  F. CRM & Business Technology
  G. Data Analytics & Performance
  H. Global Creative & Localization Services
- E and later capabilities are expansion/aspirational; do not present as fully operational until training, systems, and specialist support exist. Use consultation-based language.
- Lead capture requirements: WhatsApp enquiry button with prefilled message; Get a Quote form (name, company, location, email, contact number, required service, approximate budget, project description); consultation booking; lead notifications; lead management; mobile conversion.

## Brand Commitments

- Approved Golden K logo — represents storytelling, filmmaking, creativity, ambition, and business growth.
- Tagline: CREATE • MARKET • AUTOMATE • GROW.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real company work and client projects are the trust asset; where permission is unavailable, use illustrative artwork and label it synthetic. Do not invent testimonials, unverified international client claims, or unsupported performance guarantees.

## Product Principles

1. Client acquisition is the product; design must earn attention and action, not just look good.
2. Credibility comes from real work and honest capability claims; never fabricate proof.
3. Ambition must not cost performance: professional motion/animation only if it does not reduce loading speed or hurt Core Web Vitals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG 2.2 AA. Mobile-first responsive. Accessible navigation and forms.

## Deliberately Undecided

- Exact visual direction for the new world (brief pins: premium white background, black + metallic-gold accents, elegant modern typography, clean layout, sufficient white space; client-approved Golden K logo).
- Whether the incumbent visual implementation in `src/` (previously warm ivory / ink / electric lime direction) is preserved, extended, or replaced as part of this redesign.
